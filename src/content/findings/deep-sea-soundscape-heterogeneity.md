The observations indicate that deep-sea habitats do not share a uniform acoustic environment. Shipping strongly influenced recordings off Sanriku and in Suruga Bay, while low-frequency hydrothermal geophony characterized Suiyo Seamount. The Minamitorishima abyssal plain was comparatively quiet and had a flatter spectral structure.

Operational noise from deep-sea observation platforms substantially altered measured soundscape characteristics, particularly at offshore sites. Establishing cautious, habitat-specific acoustic baselines can therefore support future assessments of anthropogenic change, including potential effects associated with deep-sea resource extraction.
