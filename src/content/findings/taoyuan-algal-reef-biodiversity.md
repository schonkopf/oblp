The Taoyuan Algal Reef supports substantial reef-associated biodiversity despite its turbid and highly exposed intertidal setting. Ecological observations showed strong spatial heterogeneity in fish assemblages, with the Datan reef supporting particularly high fish diversity.

Soundscape observations complemented the broader ecological assessment by providing a non-invasive way to characterize biological activity where conventional underwater surveys are difficult. The acoustic data were one part of the integrated study and do not, by themselves, establish effects of coastal development on biodiversity or measure reef health.

The reef is exposed to multiple anthropogenic pressures, including coastal development, industrial and domestic pollution, and fisheries. The observed ecological variation supports continued monitoring and conservation attention across the Taoyuan Algal Reef.
