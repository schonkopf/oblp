## Built around connection

OBLP is a collaborative research framework for connecting passive acoustic observations with ecological context, reproducible analysis, and openly described evidence. Stations, surveys, publications, and resources remain linked so the network can grow without losing the context that makes observations meaningful.

## Research capabilities

Work across the observation lifecycle by combining field practice, analysis, and open infrastructure through a distributed network.

1. **Passive acoustic monitoring** — Design comparable listening programmes across fixed stations, mobile platforms, and field campaigns.
2. **Ecoacoustic analysis** — Develop transparent workflows that connect acoustic signals with environmental and biological context.
3. **Open research infrastructure** — Publish reusable metadata, methods, and discovery tools built for distributed research networks.

## Collaboration opportunities

OBLP is designed for exchange among researchers, monitoring programmes, technical teams, and communities of practice.

- **Contribute observations** — Connect compatible acoustic records and contextual measurements to a shared discovery layer.
- **Co-develop methods** — Test workflows across ecosystems, instruments, and research questions with reproducibility in mind.
- **Host a listening node** — Explore how a long-term station or time-bounded survey could join the network.
