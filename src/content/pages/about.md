  items:
    - title: "Connect an observation site"
      text: "Bring a new marine ecosystem into the listening network through long-term monitoring or spatial acoustic surveys. For research teams interested in developing soundscape observations but without established capacity, we can also visit collaborating institutions and field sites to help design monitoring strategies, establish initial observation capacity, and provide hands-on training."
    - title: "Integrate complementary ecological data"
      text: "Connect underwater soundscapes with eDNA, biological surveys, imagery, habitat mapping, Earth observation, environmental measurements, and other ecological data. These collaborations help us investigate what acoustic patterns mean ecologically and how different observation approaches can complement one another."
    - title: "Share advanced computational tools"
      text: "Work with us to analyze large acoustic datasets using signal processing, machine learning, and emerging acoustic foundation models. We are particularly interested in cross-institutional collaborations that combine acoustic observations with multimodal ecological data and develop scalable analytical workflows that can be shared across monitoring networks."
    - title: "Build regional capacity"
      text: "Develop joint workshops, training activities, student exchanges, shared protocols, and coordinated observation programmes. By connecting expertise and observations across regions, we aim to make marine ecoacoustics more accessible while improving the comparability of soundscape observations worldwide."
