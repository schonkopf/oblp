## From observation to discovery

1. **Observe** — Build long-running and campaign-scale views of ecological activity through passive acoustic monitoring.
2. **Connect** — Relate sound to habitats, environmental conditions, and complementary observations.
3. **Discover** — Develop reproducible approaches for finding and comparing meaningful patterns.

## Publication directory

Methods, evidence, and discovery remain connected. Bibliographic records link to their observation context through stable station and survey identifiers.
