The dataset contains underwater acoustic observations collected from an abyssal plain at 5,552 m depth south of Minamitorishima Island in the western North Pacific. Recordings span from March 2020 to March 2021 and provide a rare year-long acoustic record from the deep seafloor.

The observations reveal temporal variability in an environment without direct sunlight, including recurring evening fish choruses, marine mammal echolocation activity, storm-related sound, and anthropogenic noise. The dataset supports investigation of diel and seasonal biological rhythms in deep-sea soundscapes and the potential role of underwater sound in connecting surface-ocean processes with aphotic deep-sea ecosystems.
