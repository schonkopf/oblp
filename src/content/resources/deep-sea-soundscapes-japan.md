The dataset archives acoustic observations from four contrasting deep-sea ecosystems around Japan: benthic habitats off Sanriku, the twilight zone of Suruga Bay, hydrothermal vents at Suiyo Seamount, and the abyssal plain south of Minamitorishima Island.

These recordings provide a comparative acoustic baseline across deep-sea environments that differ substantially in depth, habitat characteristics, biological activity, and exposure to anthropogenic sound. The dataset was developed as part of the Ocean Biodiversity Listening Project and supports research into the use of passive acoustic monitoring for characterizing deep-sea ecosystem variability.
