Warm waters of the southern Kuroshio sustain Lyudao's well-developed reefs, but these communities also experience marine heatwaves, typhoons, episodic coral mortality, and changes in benthic composition. The station's acoustic record, complemented by temperature observations, supports assessment of how biological activity varies through seasonal and disturbance-related conditions.

The nearby 40 m station provides a mesophotic depth contrast. Together, the two persistent locations allow shallow and deeper reef dynamics to be compared without assuming that either habitat responds uniformly to environmental change.
