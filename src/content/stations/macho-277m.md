The Marine Cable Hosted Observatory (MACHO) was a fixed cabled seafloor observatory on the Ilan Ridge off northeastern Taiwan. Its environmental sensing node was located at 277 m depth, where a bottom-mounted hydrophone continuously recorded the acoustic environment from October 2011 to May 2014. The MACHO infrastructure was operated by Taiwan's Central Weather Bureau (now the Central Weather Administration).

The site lies in a biologically rich marine region shaped by complex bathymetry and the Kuroshio Current. Its long-term record contains cetacean and soniferous-fish sounds alongside abiotic and anthropogenic sources, enabling investigation of diel, lunar, and seasonal soundscape dynamics.

The submarine cable subsequently failed and the monitoring program ended. The observatory is no longer operational, and OBLP retains this record as a historical long-term observation station.
