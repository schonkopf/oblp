Northeastern Taiwan lies near the transition between tropical and subtropical marine environments. At Chaojing, rocky reef and non-reefal coral communities experience cooler winters, pronounced monsoon forcing, and strong wave exposure compared with southern Taiwan's coral reef stations.

Monitoring at 27 m ended in September 2024. The completed record retains its value as a deeper-water comparison for examining seasonal biological and environmental acoustic variability alongside the region's shallower observations.
