The station monitored a remote oceanic reef setting from September 2022 to September 2023. Its location at 11 m depth provided temporal coverage of a tropical coral reef soundscape in a geographic and ecological setting distinct from Taiwan's subtropical and nearshore monitoring sites.

The completed record supports examination of temporal variability in reef biophony, environmental sound, and anthropogenic acoustic activity. It provides historical acoustic context without presuming particular ecological patterns or conditions.
