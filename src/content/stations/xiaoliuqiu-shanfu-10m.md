Xiaoliuqiu is a coral reef island influenced by the southern Kuroshio region, yet cumulative local and climatic pressures have substantially altered many of its reefs. At Shanfu, long-term acoustic and temperature observations provide a consistent basis for following biological activity and ecosystem trajectories through changing conditions.

Because degradation varies spatially around the island, Shanfu is maintained as a distinct observation location from Lobster Cave rather than as a single island-wide reef record.
