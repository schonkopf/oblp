At 40 m, this site extends observation into a mesophotic habitat within Lyudao's southern Kuroshio-influenced reef system. Autonomous acoustic and temperature monitoring tracks seasonal and interannual variability in a depth zone that can exhibit biological sound patterns distinct from those of shallower reefs.

Comparison with Gueiwan's 20 m station allows depth-dependent ecological responses to disturbances such as marine heatwaves and typhoons to be assessed while retaining each depth as a separate physical observation location.
