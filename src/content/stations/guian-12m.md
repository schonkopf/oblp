Guian's rocky reef lies within the highly variable coastal environment of northeastern Taiwan, where seasonal cooling, the winter monsoon, and energetic waves shape the context for reef-associated sound. The station recorded both soundscape and temperature to document this variability at a persistent shallow-water location.

The autonomous monitoring program concluded in May 2026. Its completed time series remains a distinct historical record for regional comparisons with continuing stations such as Maoao.
