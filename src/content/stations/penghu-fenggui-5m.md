The station has operated at 5 m depth since May 2025. Penghu's coastal waters undergo pronounced seasonal variability associated with monsoon forcing, changing wind and wave conditions, and relatively cool winters.

Long-term passive acoustic observation provides a basis for characterizing temporal variation in biological acoustic activity, physical sound associated with wind and waves, and vessel and other anthropogenic sound. The record supports observation of the broader coastal soundscape without treating acoustic activity alone as a measure of biodiversity or ecosystem condition.
