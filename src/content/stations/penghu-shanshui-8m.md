The station has operated at 8 m depth since May 2025 within an artificial coral restoration facility in Shanshui Harbor. This managed setting is distinct from a naturally developed coral reef and occurs within Penghu's seasonally variable coastal environment.

Long-term observations provide opportunities to characterize changes in biological acoustic activity, physical environmental sound, and anthropogenic acoustic activity. They document soundscape dynamics in a restoration setting without attributing differences to restoration alone or treating acoustic activity as evidence of restoration success.
