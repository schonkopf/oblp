This fixed observation site lies in a remote western North Pacific abyssal setting south of Minamitorishima Island. A seafloor lander recorded the acoustic environment for nearly one year, from March 2020 to March 2021.

The long temporal coverage captured biological, geophysical, and anthropogenic acoustic variability at the deep seafloor. This record describes the historical observation deployment; interpretations of its temporal patterns are presented separately as a research finding.
