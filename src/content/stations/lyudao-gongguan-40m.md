This 40 m station observes a deeper portion of Gongguan's northern reef system, where biological acoustic diversity and temporal dynamics may differ from the shallows. The time series documents mesophotic soundscape and temperature variability across seasonal conditions and episodic disturbances.

Maintaining this site separately from Gongguan's 20 m station preserves the depth-resolved structure needed to compare shallow and mesophotic reef responses without treating deeper habitat as a universal refuge.
