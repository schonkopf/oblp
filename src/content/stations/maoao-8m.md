Maoao's coastal habitat supports non-reefal coral communities and diverse reef-associated fishes within a strongly seasonal setting. Repeated acoustic and temperature observations allow biological activity to be considered alongside the winter monsoon, cooler seasonal water, coastal wave energy, and human sound.

The site's shallow depth and northeastern exposure make it an important environmental counterpoint to long-term stations on the warmer coral reefs of Lyudao and Xiaoliuqiu.
