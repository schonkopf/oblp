The Lobster Cave record follows acoustic activity and temperature in a shallow reef setting where turf- and algal-dominated habitat occurs within a broader mosaic of reef conditions. Tourism, fishing, development, sedimentation, and climate-related events provide overlapping sources of environmental pressure around Xiaoliuqiu.

Long-term observation at this location complements Shanfu while recognizing that the two sites have different local contexts and need not share the same degradation trajectory.
