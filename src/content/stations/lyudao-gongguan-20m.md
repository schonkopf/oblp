Gongguan's shallow coral habitat contributes a northern-island perspective to the network of Lyudao stations. Its long-term record follows reef-associated acoustic activity and temperature variability in a region exposed to seasonal Kuroshio conditions, typhoons, and increasingly frequent climate-driven disturbances.

The corresponding 40 m location supplies a mesophotic comparison, making it possible to distinguish depth-related patterns from geographic differences elsewhere around the island.
