Published ecological research and the Coral Atlas Japan Sesoko project describe mesophotic coral habitats at Shigeo Reef, including prominent Seriatopora assemblages at some sites around 40 m and Alveopora assemblages at some deeper sites around 55 m. These observations describe regional ecological context, not acoustic survey findings or verified recording depths. They do not establish that every documented habitat occurs throughout the mapping extent.

Spatial acoustic mapping has been conducted since September 2023 to investigate how underwater soundscapes vary across reef habitats and depth gradients. The survey seeks a complementary perspective on the spatial organization of biological and environmental sounds.

The mapped rectangle is a survey envelope, not an ecological habitat boundary. The start month is confirmed; September 1 is an indexing placeholder rather than a verified fieldwork start date.
