Sinniger and colleagues' overview of mesophotic coral ecosystems around Sesoko Island describes Minna-Sone as a submerged reef system with a shallowest portion around 10 m and surrounding slopes exceeding 100 m. Their coral observations include sites around 30–40 m. These depths characterize published topographic and ecological observations, not the acoustic survey's sampling depths; the ecosystem classification does not imply a large isolated deep-ocean volcanic seamount.

Spatial acoustic mapping has been conducted since September 2023 to characterize underwater soundscape variability in this offshore environment. Differences in depth, habitat, and exposure motivate the investigation, rather than representing established acoustic results. The survey covers a habitat setting distinct from Sesoko Island's nearshore fringing reefs.

The mapped rectangle is a survey envelope, not a seafloor contour or habitat boundary. The start month is confirmed; September 1 is an indexing placeholder rather than a verified fieldwork start date.
