The coastal landscape combines varied shoreline geomorphology, oceanographic conditions, and human use. Spatial observations extend beyond fixed monitoring sites, including Chaojing, to investigate habitat-associated differences in biological sounds, physical sounds from waves and currents, and vessel or other human-generated sound. The mapping program remains distinct from those stations and the Kueishan Island surveys.

The mapped rectangle defines a survey envelope, not a habitat boundary or individual recording locations. The start month is confirmed; the first day of the month is an indexing placeholder rather than a verified deployment date.
