The three recording sites were established around Sesoko Island to compare reef soundscapes across a pronounced depth gradient. Site A was located on the southeast coast of Sesoko Island in shallow water at approximately 1.5 m depth. Site B was located north of Sesoko Island at the bottom of a reef slope at approximately 20 m depth, while Site C was located on a nearly flat plateau farther north at approximately 40 m depth.

From May 2017 to July 2018, the survey collected 19,858 hours of underwater recordings. The resulting observations captured strong spatial and temporal differences in biological and anthropogenic sounds across shallow-water and upper-mesophotic coral reef environments.
