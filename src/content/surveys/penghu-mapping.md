The survey covers contrasting coastal habitats within an archipelago influenced by seasonal monsoon conditions. Regional observations complement the independent fixed stations at Fenggui Cave and Shanshui Harbor. Their non-reefal coral communities and coral restoration setting do not characterize the entire mapping envelope, and the survey does not imply an assessment of restoration success.
