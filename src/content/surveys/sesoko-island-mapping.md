Sesoko Station's long history of coral reef research provides ecological and environmental context for observations around the island. Studies of coral communities and seasonal and long-term environmental variability inform this regional background; they are separate from the OBLP mapping observations.

Spatial acoustic mapping has been conducted since September 2023 to investigate soundscape variability across coastal fringing reef habitats. The survey seeks a spatial perspective on biological, physical, and human acoustic activity that complements the region's ecological research, without presupposing particular acoustic patterns.

This mapping survey is distinct from the earlier Sesoko comparative acoustic survey and fixed monitoring stations. The mapped rectangle is a survey envelope, not a measured coral-cover boundary. The start month is confirmed; September 1 is an indexing placeholder rather than a verified fieldwork start date.
