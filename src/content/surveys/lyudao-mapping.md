Spatial observations provide a broader coastal perspective alongside the independent shallow and mesophotic monitoring stations at Gueiwan and Gongguan. Habitat structure, exposure, and environmental conditions motivate questions about biological and environmental sound variability. Results from other Lyudao studies are background rather than findings of this mapping program, which is separate from the reef fish recruitment survey.
