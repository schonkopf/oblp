Tourism, coastal development, thermal stress, and typhoon disturbance provide context for investigating reef degradation and acoustic heterogeneity. Their effects need not be uniform throughout the survey envelope. The mapping program complements independent long-term observations at Shanfu and Lobster Cave, maintained since 2020, and remains distinct from the reef fish recruitment survey. It does not presume that acoustic observations have demonstrated reef recovery or degradation.

The mapped rectangle defines a survey envelope, not a habitat boundary or individual recording locations. The start month is confirmed; the first day of the month is an indexing placeholder rather than a verified deployment date.
